# Email Services

## Purpose

Use **Email Services** to manage accounts and templates for sending system-generated emails. Configure a sending account once, then reuse approved templates for notifications, process updates, and other outbound communications.

---

## Available Areas

| Area | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Email Accounts** | Creating and maintaining the sending profiles used by Appflexor. |
| **Email Templates** | Creating reusable message content for consistent system-generated emails. |

---

## Configure an Email Account

1. Open **Integrate → Email Services**.
2. Select the **Email Accounts** tab.
3. Click **Add New**.
4. Enter the account details required by your mail provider.
5. Save the account and confirm it is available for outbound messages.

Use a dedicated, monitored sending account for system-generated mail so business notifications are separate from individual user inboxes.

---

## Create an Email Template

1. Select the **Email Templates** tab.
2. Click **Add New**.
3. Give the template a clear name that describes when it is used.
4. Add the subject and message content, including any approved dynamic values.
5. Save the template and review a representative message before using it in a live process.

Templates help teams keep email content consistent across recurring process notifications.

---

## Maintain Email Settings

- Use **Edit** to update an account or template when requirements change.
- Use **Delete** only after confirming that no active process depends on the item.
- Review sender details and templates regularly so users receive accurate, current messages.

## Best Practices

- Use a recognizable sender name and mailbox that recipients can trust.
- Keep templates concise, actionable, and aligned with your organization’s communication standards.
- Test new templates with non-production recipients before enabling them in automated processes.
- Do not embed credentials or other sensitive data in email templates.
